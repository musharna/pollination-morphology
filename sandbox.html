about this page

Every flower here is drawn from its own genome, through the same contact model the experiments use — the shape you see is the shape that decides where its pollen lands. Two lineages start a chosen distance apart on the animal's body. Watch the field across generations: if the split held, the flowers would stay visibly two kinds.

why there is no stigma control

There is no stigma control: the model derives it from the anther. toFlower sets stigmaT to Math.min(0.92, antherT + HERKOGAMY) with HERKOGAMY 0.05 (sim/evolve.js:139) — the clamp at 0.92 included — and stigmaTheta to antherTheta. There is no placement control of any kind: placement is an outcome of two geometries meeting, never a setting.

what the body map and the bee show

Where the pollen actually goes. The same population, plotted on the animal's body: along the body left to right, around it top to bottom. This is the abstract view — it is what the model measures, and the field above is what it means. The founders are computed from the shapes above, not settable.

⚠️ The bee visits a handful of flowers per generation here. A real bout in this model is ~24,000 visits; showing them literally is impossible, so this is a sample of the bout, not the bout.
