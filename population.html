Two lineages on one bee — watching the split fail

Every plant is drawn where its pollen actually lands on the animal. Two lineages are founded a chosen distance apart on the body and then left alone. Placement is never a gene — it is computed from flower shape each generation, so what you are watching is the model's own geometry, not a scripted animation. The question the whole project turns on is visible here: do the two clouds stay apart, or drift together?

Ancestry variance is the honest read on whether the lineages are still distinct: it starts high with two pure founders and falls as they interbreed. Separation can stay high while ancestry collapses — two clusters made of the same mixed stock. That gap is exactly what the tracer was added to catch.
