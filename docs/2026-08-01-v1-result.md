The ablation found the ceiling with an **optimiser**: it saw every candidate at once and picked a
mutually compatible set. Evolution is myopic — each species can only take a step that helps it
against the community it is in right now. The gap between those two numbers is the result.

## The model

40 species seeded at random shape genomes, sharing one pollinator, 400 generations, 3 replicates.

- **Genes are shape parameters only.** Placement is recomputed from shape by the contact model on
  every evaluation. The stigma tracks the anther, offset by a herkogamy held inside the limit the
  checks found (past ~0.15 the stigma stops touching the animal at all). ⛔ **That parenthetical is
  retracted** — the stigma still contacts 81% of visits well past 0.15. A real bound exists but acts
  on the **anther**, past ~0.4. The offset v1 uses sits inside both, so nothing here changes.
  [detail](2026-08-03-checks-rebaseline.md)
- **Divergence needs a cost to misdirected pollen**, because in a single panmictic population
  placement selection is _positively_ frequency-dependent and converges. Pollen arriving on the
  wrong species' stigma is lost and occupies capacity the right pollen could have used.
- **Adaptive dynamics**, not individual-based population genetics: one mutant per species per
  generation, substituted if it does better against the current community. A real simplification.
- **Lottery recruitment** with a fixed mortality, so total occupancy is conserved.

## ⚠️ The comparison that was wrong, and the correction

Read against the τ=0.2 ceilings, evolution reaches **30%** of L2's and 37% of L1's. That number is
misleading, and the survivors say why:

```
  L2: 9 species, max pairwise overlap 0.000, mean 0.000
```

**Evolution does not pack at a tolerance. It eliminates overlap.** The optimiser was allowed to
place species 20% on top of each other; selection keeps pushing until they do not touch at all,
which costs far more room per species. So the ceiling has to be recomputed at the tolerance the
evolved communities actually exhibit:

```
  tau      L1-free   L2        (current, post-head-cap)
  0.2           12   36
  0.05           9   24
  0.01           6   18
  0              6   16
```

Against the **zero-tolerance ceiling** the honest figures are:

| arm | evolved | ceiling at τ→0 | reached |
| --- | ------- | -------------- | ------- |
| L1  | 2.7     | 6              | **44%** |
| L2  | 8.3     | 16             | **52%** |

**Evolution reaches about half of what is achievable.** Myopia costs the other half — a real
shortfall, not a collapse. _(As originally reported: 66% and 64% against ceilings of 5 and 14. The
ceilings rose once the head cap gave the optimiser the surface it had been denied, so the fraction
evolution captures fell.)_

And the ratio survives: **3.07× evolved against 2.67× for the optimiser** (16/6). The 2-D advantage
is not an artefact of having an optimiser; it is preserved under blind selection — and evolution now
sustains a slightly _larger_ advantage than the optimiser does, because the optimiser's extra room
at τ→0 helps the 1-D arm proportionally more.

## ⚠️ The first version had both controls INVERTED

L0 held all 40 species; L1 and L2 collapsed to 1 — the exact opposite of what each must do. Two
independent causes, neither a bad constant:

1. **Nothing regulated a species at high abundance.** Per-capita fitness was `own/(all+k)` with
   `own ∝ n`, so fitness rose without bound in abundance and monopoly was the only attractor,
   whatever the placement. Fixed by replacing the bare replicator with **lottery recruitment** —
   sites vacate at a fixed rate and refill in proportion to `n·r`, so a species can only take share,
   never run away.
2. **Frozen symmetry.** L0 seeded every species at exactly `1/40` with exactly equal fitness, an
   unstable equilibrium a deterministic update never leaves. It read as coexistence when it was
   symmetry never being broken. Fixed with demographic noise.

A third issue compounded the first: `k = 0.02` exceeded a species' own pollen supply at equilibrium
(`n·O_ii ≈ 0.017`), so every species was pollen-limited and the commonest simply won. `k` is now
0.0005 and the constraint is documented at the function that uses it.

Both failures are pinned in `tests/evolve.test.js` as paired controls — neither means anything
alone, since an arm that always collapses passes the L0 test and an arm that never collapses passes
the L2 test.

## ⚠️ An under-powered test read as a broken model

The L0 control initially failed at 60 generations, reporting 12 survivors. The model was fine:
**L0 reaches one species at generation 75–89**, measured across community sizes 8, 12 and 40. The
collapse is driven by noise-amplified frequency dependence and takes time. The test horizon is now
set from that measurement rather than raised until it passed.

## Limits

Adaptive dynamics with one mutant per species per generation is not population genetics — there is
no standing variation, no recombination, and no hybridisation, so this cannot speak to speciation
itself, only to coexistence among species already distinct. Species number is bounded above by the
40 seeded; the ceiling result would be firmer seeded well above 16. Overlap remains placement
overlap, not a measured transfer rate. The pollinator is one fixed body plan, though check 2 showed
the advantage holds across four.

## Verdict

**Evolution reaches ~52% of the achievable packing ceiling, and the 2-D advantage survives intact
at 3.07× against the optimiser's 2.67×.** The geometry is not merely exploitable in principle by
something with foresight — blind selection captures a substantial share of it.
