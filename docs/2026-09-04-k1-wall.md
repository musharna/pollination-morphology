# #61 — the k = 1 wall is a mating-weight zero, and she is not short of pollen

_2026-09-04. Registered in
[the pre-registration](2026-09-04-k1-wall-prereg.md). A re-analysis of #58's and
#59's archives plus one anchored re-run to measure a quantity the archives do
not store. No new cells._

## The answer in one line

At k = 1 the lone minority plant receives **4,590 grains of pollen on average and
every one of them is her own**. `received` excludes the diagonal, so her mating
weight is exactly zero, she is never drawn as a mother, and the lineage ends.
**She is not short of pollinators. She is short of partners.**

## What was asked

k = 2 is an ordinary state with an ordinary escape probability **in every arm,
including the one with no selfing at all** — 0.298 [0.175, 0.419]. k = 1 in that
same arm is 0.000 on 23 generations across 23 distinct seeds. That is a wall, not
a gradient, and #56–#60 all sit on top of it without saying what it is made of.

## The verdict on the three registered hypotheses

| hypothesis                       | prediction                             | measured                                          | verdict                            |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **H1** never pollinated          | `minMothered` = 0, receives ~0         | mothers 0 in **23/23**; receives **4,590 grains** | **prediction right, reason WRONG** |
| **H2** definitionally hybridised | mothers > 0, hybrids rise              | mothers 0; hybrids at base rate                   | **refuted**                        |
| **H3** demographic               | mothers pure young that don't compound | mothers nothing at all                            | **refuted**                        |

H2 and H3 both require her to reproduce. She does not — not rarely, **never**,
in 23 of 23 generations, CI [1.000, 1.000].

## The mechanism, measured rather than inferred

The table above shows she does not reproduce. It does not show **why**, so the
inference — that her mating weight is zero — was checked directly by re-running
arm A and reading the pollen transfer matrix. ⚠️ That re-run duplicates
`replicate()`'s loop, which is #43's failure mode, so **it carries its own
anchor**: every re-run row must reproduce the archived row for that seed or the
script refuses to report a number.

```
ANCHOR: 805 re-run rows checked against the archive, 0 disagreements

k = 1 generations measured: 23 (seeds 23)
  received (outcross pollen, the MATING WEIGHT) exactly 0 : 23/23
  SELF pollen on her stigma exactly 0                     :  0/23
  mean self-pollen received                               : 4590.13
  mean outcross received                                  : 0.0000
```

The causal chain, in live source:

1. `received[j]` sums the transfer matrix **skipping `i === j`** — the comment on
   the line is _"selfing is not mating success"_ (`sim/ibm.js:1749-1753`).
2. The mother is drawn **in proportion to `received`** (`sim/ibm.js:1793`).
3. At k = 1 a minority plant has no conspecific partner, so every grain she
   receives is her own, so `received` is **exactly 0**, so her draw weight is
   **exactly 0**.

**That is why the number is an exact zero and not a small one.** It is not a
probability that happens to be low; it is a plant with zero weight in a weighted
draw. No amount of extra seeds would move it.

## Why selfing, and only selfing, lifts it

The selfing floor is added to `weight` as `rate × mean(received)`
(`sim/ibm.js:1824`) — **independently of her own `received`**. It is the one
mechanism in the model that gives a plant with no partners a non-zero draw
weight. That is precisely the arm structure #58 and #59 measured, now with the
mechanism attached rather than inferred from the outcome.

## ⚠️ The wall I predicted was real, and it is not the one doing the work

The pre-registration derived **P1** from the source before measuring: outcrossed
offspring take `anc = (anc_m + anc_f)/2` (`:2015`), a selfed seed takes
`anc_mother` exactly (`:1952`), and `label()` is pure only at exactly 0 or 1
(`rare-floor.js:74-78`). So at k = 1 the lone plant is the only carrier of her
value and **no assignment of fathers yields a pure offspring** — a pure lineage
needs two pure parents.

P1 is true. **P1 is also doing no work**, because she never becomes a mother in
the first place, and the prereg fixed that reading in advance:

> If `minMothered` = 0 and `nh` is also ~0, then H1 and P1 are both true and P1
> is not doing the work — that combination must be reported as H1, not as H2,
> however attractive the arithmetic is.

⚠️ **This is the tell worth keeping.** A derivation that says what _cannot_
happen does not establish that the thing that failed is the thing it forbids.
There are **two nested walls** here — a mating-weight wall in front and an
averaging wall behind — and only the front one is ever tested, because nothing
gets past it. Removing the front wall alone would not produce recovery. That also
explains why selfing works so specifically: it is the unique mechanism that
defeats **both** at once, bypassing the `received` draw _and_ preserving `anc`
exactly.

Had I not registered the decision rule first, the arithmetic was attractive
enough to have been written up as the answer. It is the fifth consecutive task in
this arc where the tracer convention was the tempting explanation, and the second
where a control fixed in advance stopped it.

### ⚠️ C-base — the check that stopped me claiming a mechanism from a zero

Arm A shows `nh` = 0 in all 23 of its k = 1 generations, which reads like
reproductive isolation making a sire impossible. **It is not evidence of that.**
Cross-lineage mating does happen in arm A — 85 rows in 5 of 109 runs — so against
the arm's own base rate of 0.0223 the expected count in 23 draws is **0.51**, and
observing zero is exactly what chance predicts.

| cell      | rows | rows nh>0 | base rate | n(k=1) | expected | observed |
| --------- | ---- | --------- | --------- | ------ | -------- | -------- |
| arm A     | 3815 | 85        | 0.0223    | 23     | 0.51     | 0        |
| cost 0.00 | 3815 | 46        | 0.0121    | 69     | 0.83     | 2        |
| cost 0.25 | 3815 | 141       | 0.0370    | 91     | 3.36     | 4        |
| cost 0.50 | 3815 | 93        | 0.0244    | 74     | 1.80     | 2        |
| cost 0.75 | 3815 | 159       | 0.0417    | 49     | 2.04     | 4        |
| cost 0.95 | 3815 | 115       | 0.0301    | 40     | 1.21     | 3        |

Every arm's observed count sits at its own expectation. **The absence of hybrids
at k = 1 carries no mechanism**, and the mechanism claim rests entirely on the
measured `received` = 0 instead.

## A bonus: the inherited puzzle from #59 is answered

#59 predicted lone-generation counts would fall with cost and was **falsified** —
they run 23 / 69 / 91 / 74 / 49 / 40, rising first. #60 noted the same shape in
HOLD counts and called it a clue, not an explanation. The primary supplies the
missing piece: **the count is a dwell time.**

| cell      | k=1 gens | episodes | P(k=1 → k=1) | mean dwell | 1/(1−p) |
| --------- | -------- | -------- | ------------ | ---------- | ------- |
| arm A     | 23       | 23       | 0.000        | 1.000      | 1.000   |
| cost 0.00 | 69       | 51       | 0.261        | 1.353      | 1.353   |
| cost 0.25 | 91       | 56       | 0.389        | 1.625      | 1.636   |
| cost 0.50 | 74       | 51       | 0.311        | 1.451      | 1.451   |
| cost 0.75 | 49       | 41       | 0.163        | 1.195      | 1.195   |
| cost 0.95 | 40       | 38       | 0.050        | 1.053      | 1.053   |

Dwell is **geometric in P(stay at k = 1)** to three decimals in five of six
cells. Selfing raises that probability, inbreeding depression lowers it, and the
product with the number of episodes entered produces the rise-then-fall. In arm A
P(stay) is 0.000 and dwell is exactly 1.000 — she cannot linger, because she
cannot reproduce.

⚠️ The correlation across cells is 0.989, but **n = 6 cells, not seeds**. It is
reported as descriptive and cannot be promoted without a seed-level design.

⚠️ **A bug in the first draft of this table is worth recording**: conditioning
P(k=1 → k=1) on the next row being `informative` conditions on **survival** — a
row goes non-informative exactly when a pure count hits zero, which is the exit
being counted. Arm A, which always exits, returned `NaN` and vanished from the
table. The conditioning-set builder was extracted to
`experiments/k1-episodes.js` with that trap and #60's minority-fixing trap under
test, and both were seen to fail on a mutant.

## Limitations

1. **Re-analysis plus one anchored re-run**, inheriting #58/#59's design: N0 = 30,
   one premium setting, rate 2.0 for every cost arm.
2. **Arm A's k = 1 evidence is 23 generations.** They are 23 distinct seeds, and
   the measured quantity is an exact structural zero rather than a rate, but the
   conditioning set is still small.
3. **`received` was measured for arm A only.** The selfing arms' mechanism is
   read from the source (`:1824`) and from `minMothered`, not from a matrix.
4. **Her role as a FATHER is not in the archives.** `minMothered` counts mothers;
   whether the lone plant ever sires is not recoverable from the stored fields.
   It cannot change the pure-offspring conclusion — a hybrid results either way —
   but it is not measured.
5. **The dwell result is across 6 cells**, descriptive only.
6. **P1 remains untested**, and by construction cannot be tested while the
   mating-weight wall stands in front of it.
