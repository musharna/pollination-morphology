Two flowers, one bee — where does the pollen land?

Grey box. Nothing here is styled to look like anything. Both flowers are visited by the same animal; the only difference between them is the position of the anther. Drag to rotate.

shared placement
Placement is never an input. There is no parameter anywhere that says where pollen goes. The sites are computed from tube shape, body shape and how far the animal can push in. What this does not model yet: carryover between visits, packaging efficiency, last-male advantage, pollinator learning, or any reward at all — all enumerated, all deferred past v0. The number above is placement overlap, not a transfer rate.
